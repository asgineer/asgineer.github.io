About Me

Engineer by training, maker by passion.

I'm a mechanical engineer with a deep fascination for robotics, mechatronics, and intelligent systems. My work revolves around bridging the gap between theoretical engineering and hands-on, real-world implementations — whether it's a tendon-driven exoskeleton, a custom-built CNC machine, or a weekend hiking adventure.

During my academic years I explored control systems, biomechanics, and human-robot interaction. Outside the lab I love tinkering — building things with my hands, travelling to new places, and writing about whatever catches my curiosity.

This site is a living record of what I build, where I go, and what I learn along the way.
