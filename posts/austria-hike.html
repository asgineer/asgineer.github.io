Hiking the Dachstein Glacier Trail — A Weekend in the Austrian Alps

📅 September 1, 2023 ⏱ 6 min read ✏️ asgineer

The Dachstein massif sits on the border of Styria and Upper Austria, rising to 2,995 m — and for a long weekend in late August, it was precisely what I needed. Glaciers, summit ridges, jaw-dropping views, and huts that serve the most satisfying Kaiserschmarrn you've ever tasted.

The Route

I chose the classic 3-day Dachstein circuit, starting and ending in Ramsau am Dachstein:

  • Day 1: Ramsau → Simonyhütte (2,206 m) via the Gosausee gondola. ~4 h walking.
  • Day 2: Simonyhütte → Gjaidalm → Wiesberghaus. Crossing the Hallstätter Glacier moraine. ~6 h.
  • Day 3: Wiesberghaus → Ramsau descent through the Wald via Hoher Dachstein viewpoint. ~3.5 h.

Day 1: Arrival and the Blue Lake

I took a morning train from Vienna to Stainach-Irdning and a regional bus to Ramsau. The gondola to Krippenstein cuts out 1,000 m of vertical climb, leaving you right at the edge of the karst plateau. From there it's a 2-hour walk to Simonyhütte along the Gjaidstein ridge with the Hallstätter Glacier filling the valley below.

The hut is perched above the Seethalsee — a cobalt-blue glacial lake that reflects the surrounding limestone walls. Book ahead; it fills up every evening in summer.

Day 2: Across the Glacier Moraine

This is the most spectacular day. The route crosses the ancient moraine left by the retreating Hallstätter Glacier — massive boulders, ice-polished slabs, and the eerie silence of a high alpine environment. Navigation requires attention: the cairns are spaced widely and morning fog can roll in fast.

At 2,400 m, with nothing but ridgeline above and an empty valley below, there is a kind of clarity you cannot find anywhere else.

Gear I Used

  • Salomon X Ultra 4 mid GTX — waterproof, supportive on loose rock
  • Black Diamond Cirque 45 pack
  • Lightweight down parka (even in August, nights drop to 2–4 °C)
  • Garmin inReach Mini 2 — peace of mind above 2,000 m
  • Trekking poles — invaluable on the moraine descent

Practical Info

  • Season: July–September. Glacier sections may require crampons in June.
  • Difficulty: Moderate–Demanding (T4 on the SAC scale).
  • Huts: Simonyhütte (+43 6131 8295), Wiesberghaus (+43 6131 8290).
  • Getting there: Train to Stainach-Irdning, then bus to Ramsau. No car needed.

If you're an engineer who needs a weekend reminder that not everything is optimisable — go to the Dachstein. Highly recommended.
